How a Guaranteed Rental Return Works

The idea is the same for every property here. The details differ, so always read each one's terms.

  1. 1You buy a unitA hotel suite, resort villa or furnished studio, and you sign a rental agreement with the developer.
  2. 2An operator runs itA hotel or short-stay company rents it to guests and looks after it. You don't find tenants.
  3. 3You get paid rentA fixed rent for the first years, promised in writing. After that, usually a share of the profit.
First years

Guaranteed rent

A fixed percentage of the price each year, for a set number of years, whatever the hotel earns.

After that

Profit-sharing or your choice

Rent depends on the hotel's results, or you leave the programme and use, rent out or sell the unit yourself.

5 Questions to Ask Before You Buy Any GRR Property

  1. Who pays the guaranteed rent, and can they afford it? It is a promise from the developer, so check its track record.
  2. For how many years, and from when? Rent often starts only after the building is finished.
  3. Is the rate before or after running costs? In the developers' own examples on this site, maintenance, sinking fund and taxes take about 0.4% to 0.9% a year off.
  4. What happens when the guarantee ends? Ask for the profit-sharing terms and what the hotel next door actually earned.
  5. Can you leave, use or sell the unit? Check the notice period and whether selling stops the rent.

Questions People Ask

What is a guaranteed rental return (GRR)?

It is a promise from the developer to pay you a fixed rent for a set number of years after you buy, whatever the hotel or tenants actually earn. It is written into a rental agreement you sign with the developer.

Is a guaranteed rental return safe?

It is only as safe as the developer paying it. Check who pays, for how long, whether the rate is before or after running costs, and what happens if the developer cannot pay. Every property page on this site lists its risks.

Why do developers offer a guaranteed rent?

It makes a hotel-style property easier to sell, because buyers know what rent to expect in the first years. The cost of the guarantee can be built into the price, so compare the price per square foot with similar finished units.

What happens when the guarantee ends?

Usually you move into a profit-sharing programme, where your rent depends on how well the hotel does, or you leave the programme and use, rent out or sell the unit yourself. The terms differ for each property.

Can foreigners buy these properties?

Yes, above a minimum price set by each state. Check the current rule for the state the property is in before you commit.

Does GRR Malaysia charge buyers?

No. We are paid a commission by the agency or developer when a sale completes. It does not increase the price you pay.
