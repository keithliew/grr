How It Works

Three simple steps, then two stages of rent.

  1. 1You buy a villaYou sign the sale agreement and join the rental programme.
  2. 2The resort runs itYour villa becomes a resort room. Lexis Hotel Group fills it with guests and looks after it.
  3. 3You get paidThe developer pays you rent every 3 months for the first 3 years.
Years 1 to 3

8% a year, guaranteed

Paid every 3 months. You pay running costs of about RM9,000 a year, so about 7.1% is left. Or choose 6.5% a year paid early, during building, with costs covered.

Years 4 to 15

A share of resort profit

Owners share 75% of the resort's profit, paid every month. The developer and operator keep 25%. The amount goes up and down with the resort's business.

What these words mean
Guaranteed rent
Rent the developer promises in writing for a set number of years, whatever the hotel earns. Often called GRR (guaranteed rental return).
Estimate
The developer's own forecast. It is not a promise and can turn out lower or higher.
Running costs
The maintenance fee, the sinking fund (savings for big repairs), quit rent and assessment (yearly land and council taxes), and insurance.
Profit-sharing
After the guaranteed years, owners split the hotel's profit instead of getting a fixed rent.
Gross and net
Gross is before running costs are taken off. Net is what is left after.

The Risks, in Plain Words

Every investment has risks. These are the ones that matter most here.

  • The promise is only as good as the developer. The guaranteed rent depends on the developer being able to pay.
  • After year 3, nothing is promised. Your share depends on how busy the resort is. The resort next door paid 6.35% in 2024.
  • The estimate needs a busier resort. It assumes 60% to 67% full; next door was 53.28% full in 2024.
  • It isn't built yet. Handover is forecast for June 2029 and could be late.
  • It is tied to tourism. Travel slowdowns, like during a pandemic, can cut income.

Questions People Ask

Is the 8% really guaranteed?

Yes, for the first 3 years, paid every 3 months. You can choose 6.5% a year after costs, paid in advance while the resort is built, instead.

What happens after year 3?

Owners share 75% of the resort's profit to year 15, paid monthly. The developer estimates an 11.5% average before costs over 15 years. This is not promised.

How much does a villa cost?

From RM774,000. Type A villas average about RM1,000,000. The booking fee is RM2,000, refunded when your loan is paid out.

What will I pay each year?

About RM8,988 to RM9,090 a year for a Type A villa, covering maintenance, sinking fund, insurance and land and council tax.

Can foreigners buy?

Yes, above a minimum price set by each state. Confirm the current amount for Negeri Sembilan before you commit.

Can I stay in my villa?

Owners get 10 free nights a year. The villa is let to resort guests the rest of the time.

Can I leave the rental programme?

Yes. You can end the rental agreement with 3 months' written notice to the developer.

When is it ready?

The developer forecasts handover in June 2029.
