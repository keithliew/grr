How the 6% Works

The developer has confirmed to GRR Malaysia that Residensi Platinum comes with a 6% per annum guaranteed rental return. The developer shares the full written terms only at a showroom appointment, so this page does not state the guarantee period or payment method yet.

The developer's printed advertisement calls the 6% an “indicative ROI”. Before you pay a booking fee, ask to see the guarantee written into the sale and purchase agreement or a separate rental agreement.

Questions to ask at the appointment

  • How many years is the 6% guaranteed, and from when (vacant possession or earlier)?
  • Is 6% gross or net of maintenance, sinking fund and management fees?
  • Is it paid monthly, quarterly, or given as a rebate on the price?
  • What happens to rental income after the guarantee ends?
  • Can you opt out of the programme and use the unit yourself?

Risks, Stated Plainly

  • A guarantee is only as strong as the party paying it. Check who pays (developer or operator) and what happens if they can't.
  • The price may include the cost of the guarantee. Compare the price per sq ft with similar completed units nearby.
  • Income after the guarantee depends on demand. Short-stay rules in Kuala Lumpur and competition from nearby units affect real occupancy.
  • The project is under construction. Completion is expected in December 2028; delays are possible.
  • Leasehold. The 99-year lease runs to 2118.
