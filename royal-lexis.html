How the Rental Programme Works

Years 1–3: Guaranteed

6% per annum, guaranteed (18% over 3 years), paid quarterly in arrears. You lease the suite back to the developer, Kuala Lumpur Metro Property Sdn Bhd, under a 3-year tenancy agreement. The draft agreement runs from 1 December 2030 to 30 November 2033, or from 3 months after the certificate of completion, whichever is later.

The developer pays your service charge, sinking fund, quit rent, assessment, insurance and sewerage from the rent and pays you the balance. On the sales kit's example that is about 5.6% net.

Years 4–15: Profit-sharing

Paid monthly. 75% of the hotel's net operating profit is shared among owners in the rental scheme in proportion to purchase price; 25% goes to the developer and operator. Owners in the programme get 10 free nights a year (terms apply).

Rent in advance during construction

The sales kit shows an option to take part of the guaranteed rent early to reduce three construction instalments. In its example on a RM2,000,000 suite (RM1,900,000 after the 5% discount), RM85,500 of each of instalments 2, 3 and 4 is covered as advance rental, so the buyer pays RM1,643,500 in instalments. Ask how rent taken early changes the rent paid after completion.

Who Royal Lexis Suits

Royal Lexis is a hotel investment, not a home. It suits buyers who want a hands-off income asset in central Kuala Lumpur and can wait for it.

City-centre hotel income without running it

Lexis Hotel Group runs the hotel, and in the guaranteed years the developer pays your service charge, sinking fund and taxes from the rent. You don't find tenants or handle bookings.

Buyers who can hold for 10 to 15 years

The programme runs 15 years. On the developer's projections, total rent passes the purchase price in about year 14. This is a long-term holding, not a quick resale.

A freehold address in KL's hotel district

Freehold land on Jalan Sultan Ismail, 50 m from Bukit Nanas Monorail and 1 km from KLCC. The operator expects most guests from online bookings and walk-ins (38%) and tour groups (32%), with corporate, government and meetings making up the rest.

Overseas buyers who want a managed asset

Rent is paid to you, the hotel is run for you, and owners get 10 free nights a year when visiting. Foreign buyers must meet Malaysia's foreign-ownership rules for Kuala Lumpur; check the current threshold first.

Probably not for you if…

  • you want a home to live in or a place to stay often: the suite is let as a hotel room, with 10 free nights a year for owners;
  • you need rental income before December 2030;
  • you need the rent to cover a loan every single year: after year 3 it depends on hotel profit, and force majeure can pause it;
  • you need a car park or a residential title;
  • your budget is below about RM1.9 million.

Risks, Stated Plainly

  • The guarantee is the developer's promise. It is paid by Kuala Lumpur Metro Property Sdn Bhd and depends on its ability to pay.
  • Force majeure. The draft tenancy agreement relieves the developer of its obligations during events beyond its control, including pandemics, lockdowns and natural disasters.
  • Rent stops in two cases: if your bank auctions the unit after a loan default, or if you sell it without the developer's written consent.
  • Years 4–15 depend on hotel profit. The projections assume occupancy rising from 58% to 69%.
  • Commercial title. Loan margins, interest and running costs can differ from a residential property.
  • Under construction. Completion is forecast for September 2030; delays are possible.
  • No car park comes with the unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Royal Lexis have a guaranteed rental return?

Yes. The developer's sales kit (14 October 2026) states a guaranteed rental return of 6% per annum for the first 3 years (18% in total), paid quarterly in arrears under a tenancy agreement with Kuala Lumpur Metro Property Sdn Bhd. The draft tenancy agreement runs for 3 years, from 1 December 2030 to 30 November 2033.

Is the 6% before or after maintenance fees?

Before. The tenancy agreement says the developer pays your service charge, sinking fund, quit rent, assessment, insurance and sewerage out of the rent and pays you the balance. The sales kit's own example shows 6% gross becoming about 5.6% a year after those outgoings (RM112,216 a year on an average RM2,000,000 Type A unit).

What happens after the first 3 years?

For the remaining 12 years of the 15-year programme, rent is paid monthly on a profit-sharing basis: 75% of the hotel's net operating profit goes to owners in the rental scheme, in proportion to purchase price, and 25% to the developer and operator. The sales kit projects 6.03% in year 4 rising to 9.45% in year 15, based on occupancy rising from 58% to 69%. These are projections, not guarantees.

How much does a unit cost?

The sales kit lists prices from RM1,897,000 to RM4,418,000, from about RM2,950 per sq ft. Units in the rental programme get a 5% discount; units bought for own use without the programme get 10%. The booking fee is RM20,000.

What sizes are available?

371 hotel suites on levels 11 to 45: Type A 573 sq ft (112 units), Type B 802 sq ft (70 units), Type C 1,052 to 1,085 sq ft with a dual-key layout (175 units) and Type D 1,155 sq ft (14 units). Every unit has its own private pool and comes fully furnished. No car park is included.

When will it be completed?

Construction started in April 2025. The sales kit forecasts completion in September 2030; the sale and purchase agreement allows 54 months from the agreement date for vacant possession.

Is it freehold?

Yes. The land title (Geran 79546, Lot 20023 Seksyen 57, Bandar Kuala Lumpur) is freehold, and its express condition limits use to a commercial building for serviced apartments, hotel, restaurants, business space, cafés and parking. This is a commercial title, not a residential one, which can affect loan terms and costs.

Can I leave the rental programme?

The draft tenancy agreement lets the owner end it with at least 3 months' written notice. The sales kit also offers owners in the programme 10 free nights a year (terms apply).
