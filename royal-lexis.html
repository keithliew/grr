How It Works

Three simple steps, then two stages of rent.

  1. 1You buy a suiteYou sign a sale agreement and a rental agreement with the developer at the same time.
  2. 2The hotel runs itYour suite becomes a hotel room. Lexis Hotel Group fills it with guests and looks after it.
  3. 3You get paidThe developer pays you rent. It first takes off your running costs, so you don't pay bills.
Years 1 to 3

6% a year, guaranteed

Paid every 3 months, from December 2030 to November 2033. The developer pays your running costs out of it, so about 5.6% reaches you.

Years 4 to 15

A share of hotel profit

Owners share 75% of the hotel's profit, paid every month. The developer and the hotel keep 25%. The amount goes up and down with the hotel's business.

What these words mean
Guaranteed rent
Rent the developer promises in writing for a set number of years, whatever the hotel earns. Often called GRR (guaranteed rental return).
Estimate
The developer's own forecast. It is not a promise and can turn out lower or higher.
Running costs
The maintenance fee, the sinking fund (savings for big repairs), quit rent and assessment (yearly land and council taxes), and insurance.
Profit-sharing
After the guaranteed years, owners split the hotel's profit instead of getting a fixed rent.
Gross and net
Gross is before running costs are taken off. Net is what is left after.

The Risks, in Plain Words

Every investment has risks. These are the ones that matter most here.

  • The promise is only as good as the developer. The guaranteed rent depends on Kuala Lumpur Metro Property Sdn Bhd being able to pay.
  • Rent can pause in a crisis. The rental agreement lets the developer stop paying during events it can't control, like a pandemic or a lockdown.
  • Rent stops in two cases: if your bank takes the suite because the loan isn't paid, or if you sell without the developer's written OK.
  • After year 3, nothing is promised. Your share depends on how busy the hotel is.
  • It is a commercial title. Bank loans and costs can be different from a normal home.
  • It isn't built yet. Handover is forecast for September 2030 and could be late.
  • No car park comes with the suite.

Questions People Ask

Is the 6% really guaranteed?

Yes, for the first 3 years. It is written into the rental agreement with the developer, Kuala Lumpur Metro Property Sdn Bhd. The draft agreement runs from 1 December 2030 to 30 November 2033.

Is the 6% before or after costs?

Before. The developer takes off your maintenance fee, sinking fund, taxes and insurance, then pays you the rest. On the developer's example that is about 5.6% a year.

What happens after year 3?

Owners share 75% of the hotel's profit for 12 more years, paid monthly. The developer estimates 6.03% in year 4 rising to 9.45% in year 15. This is not promised.

How much does a suite cost?

From RM1,897,000 to RM4,418,000. You get 5% off if you join the rental programme. The booking fee is RM20,000.

Can I stay in my suite?

Owners in the rental programme get 10 free nights a year. It is a hotel room the rest of the time.

Can I leave the rental programme?

Yes. The draft rental agreement lets you end it with 3 months' written notice.

Is it freehold?

Yes. The land title is freehold and approved for serviced apartments and hotel use. That makes it a commercial title, not a residential one.

When is it ready?

The developer forecasts September 2030. Building started in April 2025.
